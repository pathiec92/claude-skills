# Leadership Lens

Assess organizational and long-term impact — not just the technical mechanics.
A locally optimal choice can be globally expensive.

## Evaluate
- Team productivity & cognitive load
- Long-term ownership & maintenance burden
- Organizational impact & blast radius
- Cross-team dependencies & coordination cost

## Suggest
- Architecture governance (guardrails, golden paths)
- Platform opportunities (build once, reuse across teams)
- Standardization vs justified divergence
- Technical-debt reduction with a business case

## Framing
- Tie technical choices to delivery speed, reliability, and cost
- Make the trade-off explicit: what does this optimize for, and at whose expense?
